# Asset descriptions — GOEVO (no-capture path, brand assets fetched directly)

Full browser capture of goevo.com.br was blocked by the site's WAF
(net::ERR_CONNECTION_RESET for headless browsers). Brand tokens and logos were
fetched directly over HTTP from the site's public CSS/uploads instead.

## capture/assets/goevo-logo-branco.png

Official GOEVO logo, white version for dark backgrounds — 724×453 PNG with
transparency. White "GoEvo" wordmark inside a white cloud outline, with the
brand's tri-color status bar underneath (red `#ED1C24` → green `#00AA4C` →
orange `#F8951D`, rounded pill segments). Use on dark/blue grounds; the final
logo reveal of the video.

## capture/assets/goevo-logo-quadrado.png

Official square GOEVO icon, 192×192 PNG — blue cloud (bright blue `#0099FF`)
containing the "GoEvo" wordmark in dark brand blue (`#00489E`), tri-color bar
underneath. Colored version for light backgrounds; usable as a compact brand
mark (avatar-sized).

## Brand notes

- Elementor global colors from the live site: primary `#00489E` (deep blue),
  secondary/accent `#0099FF` (bright blue), text `#191919`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- All site typography: Montserrat.
- The tri-color pill bar (red/green/orange) is a distinctive brand element —
  echoes "status" (blocked/ok/pending); good for the mock-UI status colors.
- No product screenshots were captured; UI shown in the video must be mock UI
  with fictitious data (client requirement — no client names/e-mails).
- Client logos on the site must NOT be used.
